Shared foundation

  • CLI remains the sole source of measurements, thresholds, and diagnosis prose.
  • Expert detail stays available without crowding the default path.

Key trade-off

  • Status-first is fastest to ship.
  • Change-first is strongest for intermittent problems.
  • Guided check has the clearest action loop but needs more interaction design.
